Distill a task's raw output for consumption by downstream tasks.

Compression preserves factual content and structured data while
removing redundancy. The strategy emerges from reasoning about
what downstream tasks need.

Rules:
- ONLY distill from raw_output. Do NOT invent, infer, or hallucinate
  content that is not present in the raw output.
- Preserve exact file paths, URLs, error messages, code, and numbers.
- Preserve structured data (JSON, tables, lists) in their original format.
- When downstream_tasks is provided, prioritize information those tasks
  would need (file paths they'll read, APIs they'll call, data they'll process).
