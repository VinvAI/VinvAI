# Follow along in the Flow panel

The Flow panel in the Vinv sidebar is the whole product on one rail:

- **Discover** — the project handbook and the interactive code map
- **Services** — how each part of your project starts, verified
- **Traces** — what actually ran, request by request
- **Insights** — where time went: call trees, flamegraphs, health reports
- **Verify** — does it work? Problems land here in red, each with a **Fix with agent** button

Everything is one click from this panel. When something needs you, a single **Next step** card says what and why — no hunting through menus.

Prefer chatting? **Ask Vinv** (comment icon at the top of the panel) answers questions about your codebase from the map plus real runtime evidence, with citations.
